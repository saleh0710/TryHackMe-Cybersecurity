- Learned how DNS translates domain names into machine-readable addresses.
- Reviewed the role of recursive resolvers, nameservers, and domain records.
- Recognised the importance of DNS for navigation and service discovery.
- Reflected on how DNS failures can affect websites and online services.

### HTTP in Detail
- Learned how HTTP requests and responses work in the browser-server model.
- Studied methods such as GET and POST and how they affect application behaviour.
- Reviewed status codes and how they communicate success, redirection, and errors.
- Learned that HTTP is a foundational protocol for modern web communication.

### How Websites Work
- Learned how a website is composed of client-side and server-side components.

### Putting It All Together

- Reflected on the importance of understanding web basics before working with web security.
- Recognised how debugging network and web issues requires a layered mental model.

## Cyber Security Basics

### The CIA Triad
- Learned the core security principles of confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
- Studied how these three objectives guide secure design and risk management.
- Reflected on how real-world security decisions often involve trade-offs between them.
- Recognised the importance of balancing protection with usability and business needs.

### Cryptography Concepts
- Learned the basic goals of cryptography: confidentiality, integrity, and authentication.
- Studied common concepts such as encryption, hashing, and keys.
- Reviewed the difference between symmetric and asymmetric approaches.
- Reflected on the role of cryptography in protecting data and validating trust.

### Become a Hacker
- Learned that “hacking” in a cybersecurity context often means systematic exploration and testing.
- Studied the mindset of structured investigation, enumeration, and problem solving.
- Reviewed the importance of ethical boundaries and permission before testing environments.
- Reflected on the value of understanding attacker methods to strengthen defence.

### Become a Defender
- Learned how defensive security focuses on prevention, detection, and response.
- Reviewed the role of monitoring, logging, access control, and system hardening.
- Recognised how understanding attacker behaviour improves protective strategies.
- Reflected on the need to combine technical controls with security awareness and good practices.
