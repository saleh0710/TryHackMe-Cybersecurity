# Client-Server Basics

This topic explores how client devices and servers work together to provide digital services.

## What I Learned

- Clients send requests and servers provide responses.
- Requests and responses rely on protocols, addresses, and service endpoints.
- Understanding the relationship between clients and servers is essential for network and web work.

## Reflection

Client-server communication is one of the core ideas behind the internet and all application systems. Learning it gave me a clearer picture of how digital services operate.
