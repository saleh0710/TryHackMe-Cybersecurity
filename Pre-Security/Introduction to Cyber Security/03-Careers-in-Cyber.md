# Careers in Cyber

This topic explored the breadth of cybersecurity careers and the range of roles available in the field.

## What I Learned

- Cybersecurity includes technical, analytical, and communication-based roles.
- Different roles support different parts of the security lifecycle.
- Technical understanding of systems, networks, and software is valuable across many career paths.

## Reflection

This module helped me see that cybersecurity is a broad field with many specialisations. A strong foundation in networking, systems, and security concepts is essential for growth in this area.
