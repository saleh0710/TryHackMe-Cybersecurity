# Cryptography Concepts

This topic introduces the basic ideas behind cryptography and why it is essential for secure communication and storage.

## What I Learned

- Cryptography protects confidentiality and integrity.
- Encryption, hashing, and keys are central concepts.
- Symmetric and asymmetric methods serve different security goals.

## Reflection

Cryptography is not just a theoretical topic; it is a practical foundation for trust, identity, and protected communication in digital systems.
